Card-Lending Scale Strategy

Two acquisition targets, one capital budget. Project Northfield buys a $7.6bn revolving card book outright; Project Harborview buys the underwriting capability and the deposits to fund it. This brief sets out what the pro forma numbers say about each.

Acquire Harborview Community Bank for $1,050mm. Decline Northfield as structured.

Proceed

Northfield is the more accretive transaction and the one that answers the board's mandate in a single step. It is also not executable as structured. It takes pro forma CET1 to 7.42% — 358bp below our internal target and 42bp above the regulatory floor — and forces a $1,054.5mm equity issuance equal to a fifth of our market capitalisation. Price the capital in, apply the board's recession stress, and Northfield's return advantage over Harborview collapses from 858bp to 67bp. We would be paying a full price, in scarce capital, for a book whose credit quality has deteriorated in each of the last three years.

What the board is asked to approve

Proceed — Harborview, subject to conditions

Eight conditions; the four that carry the most weight

  1. Capital. Buybacks suspended from signing until CET1 clears 11.0% — expected within two quarters from retained earnings, so no issuance is required.
  2. Underwriting talent. Two-year retention for the Chief Credit Officer and the full consumer underwriting team, as a condition precedent. The capability is the asset being bought.
  3. Funding synergy validation. The 10bp blended funding improvement — $45.6mm, the largest single synergy line — evidenced against the actual funding book before signing, with price revisited below 7bp.
  4. Walk-away discipline. No consideration above $1,050mm without returning to the board. At $1,150mm the Year-3 return on committed capital falls below 13%.

Decline — Northfield, with a route back

Four conditions for any re-engagement

  1. Price. Maximum $1,320mm — standalone value, no control premium. The current $1,650mm asks our shareholders to pay a premium for risk transfer running the wrong way.
  2. Structure. At least 64% of consideration in Meridian stock. Newly issued acquirer equity counts as CET1 and offsets the goodwill deduction; it is the only structure that avoids a separate $1bn issuance.
  3. Credit stabilisation. Two consecutive half-years of flat or improving 90+ DPD and charge-offs, with independently re-performed vintage loss curves for the FY2024–25 cohorts.
  4. Conduct & capital. Written confirmation the CLI remediation is retired, plus indemnity and escrow — and a pre-funded capital plan approved before signing, not after.
